Home store

Klein

Furniture, objects and records from the studio that designed Signal.

Home Store by Klein opened in June 2025. Klein Agency, run by Jon and Maša Kleinhample, designed the Signal building itself.

Their own pieces sit next to vintage finds, art, music and books. Wooden bowls, glass plates, sandcast aluminium hardware, crystal from Slovenia.

The aluminium shelving and its built-in audio are designed, made and finished in Los Angeles.
