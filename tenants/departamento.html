Departamento

Designers, clothing, footwear and accessories. Enter through Concierge Coffee.

A designer clothing shop, known as DPTO.

Clothing, footwear and accessories from independent labels. The way in is through the coffee bar next door.

A second shop, DPTO Annex, sits on South Santa Fe Avenue.

Worth coming for

  1. The labels

    Loewe, The Row, Lemaire, Dries Van Noten, Martine Rose, Wales Bonner, Kiko Kostadinov, Our Legacy and visvim, among others.

  2. Taiga Takahashi room

    A shop in the shop, set up like a ryokan with cedar and tatami.

  3. The way in

    A hidden door in Concierge Coffee, then a mirrored walkway.

  4. The room itself

    Designed by the Los Angeles studio 22RE, with sculpted display blocks and private fitting rooms.
