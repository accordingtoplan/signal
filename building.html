The building

821 Traction Ave

A 1910 warehouse, opened up around a courtyard. White stucco, arched windows and a bottle tree in the middle. Every shop opens onto it. Stairs lead up to a roof deck over downtown.

How it came together

  1. 1910

    The warehouse

    Built for industry on Traction Ave. 25,000 square feet. Most recently split into 15 creative offices.

  2. 2019

    Free Market

    Raan Parton and Paolo Carini open Free Market, a shop of shops in Denver's Dairy Block. It works. The next step is a building of their own.

  3. 2022

    Traction Ave

    The partners take on the warehouse, first planned as another Free Market. It becomes Signal, a destination that stands on its own.

  4. 2024

    Signal opens

    Klein Agency keeps the old structure and builds on it. 40,000 square feet over two floors, with a modern nod to the California missions. Shops downstairs, Greycroft upstairs.

Who is behind it

  1. 01

    Raan Parton

    Co-founded the label Apolis with his brother, Shea. Started Alchemy Works with his wife, Lindsay, in 2013. Its first Arts District store was around the corner.

  2. 02

    Paolo Carini

    Worked as a lawyer first. Then two decades with fashion family offices in Milan, New York and London. Partner with Raan Parton in Stardust Partners.

  3. 03

    Klein Agency

    Jon and Masa Kleinhample's studio designed the building. Their own shop is in the courtyard.
